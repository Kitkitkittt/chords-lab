# ChordsLab Learning

Language for a learner's journey through a musical concept and the evidence that journey produces.

## Language

**Concept cycle**:
The repeatable journey through Orient, Learn, Retrieve, Practice, Apply, Review later, and choosing the next concept, anchored by one lesson and its skill targets.

**Foreground concept**:
The one concept currently guiding the primary path.

**Retention tail**:
A concept's later-review obligation after foreground handoff.

**Learning evidence**:
A recorded interaction classified as unaided, assisted, practice, or later review.
_Avoid_: Mastery

**Interrupted work**:
Committed in-progress state at a semantic boundary.
_Avoid_: Last-opened route
